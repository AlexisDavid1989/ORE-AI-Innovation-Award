**Quick question: what's the coolest thing you could build with AI for ORE, if someone actually gave you a reason to?**

Good news: the **ORE AI Innovation Award 2026** just opened, and it's exactly the excuse you needed. ORE — our Open Source Risk Engine — turns 10 this year, and we're marking it with an award for the best AI-powered thing you can build around it. Doesn't matter if it's a tool that makes ORE easier to use, a smarter way to work with its outputs, or something that just makes life better for people who touch ORE every day. Open to everyone across Post-Trade Solutions, seasoned dev or not.

You don't even need to pick a category — every project gets entered into the main categories automatically. Crowd Favorite is decided by votes, so yes, you're allowed to shamelessly ask your team to back you. If you want to enter J.A.R.V.I.S.'s Pick too, that's optional — and it rewards the smartest use of AI tooling and prompting.

A few reasons to actually do this instead of just thinking about it:
- Recognition in front of leadership, plus a certificate you can put on your wall
- A great way to learn what AI tools can actually do, hands-on, with a real use case
- A chance to learn how ORE actually works under the hood, in a fun way
- Nothing sparks imagination like a bit of friendly competition

Deadline is **October 18, 2026, 23:59 UK time**. That sounds far away. It isn't.

- [Award website]({{WEBSITE_LINK}}) — the pitch, categories, key dates
- [SharePoint page]({{SHAREPOINT_LINK}}) — how to enter, FAQ, submission kit

Ten years of ORE deserves a proper celebration. If you've already got an idea, reply below — curious what people are cooking up.
