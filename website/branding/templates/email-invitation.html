Best AI Creation for ORE | September 2026

Hello Post-Trade Solutions team,

You're invited to take part in the ORE AI Innovation Award 2026, a September competition — open to all Post-Trade Solutions employees — to recognize the best AI creation built for ORE. Fly solo or bring a team; you can enter more than one project.

About the Award

This is a chance to showcase work that is original, useful, visually strong, or simply the most compelling AI idea for the ORE ecosystem, as part of ORE's 10-year anniversary celebrations. Winners will be announced sometime in November.

Everyone builds with GitHub Copilot, and a local, zero-token script captures your usage automatically — nothing to track by hand.

Key Dates

Applications Open: September 14, 2026

Applications Close: September 30, 2026, 23:59 UK time

Winners Announced: Sometime in November 2026, as part of ORE's 10-year anniversary celebrations

Award Categories

  • AI Game Changer Award — The strongest all-around entry
  • Boldest AI Idea Award — The most original concept and biggest creative leap
  • Customer Wow Award — The best-looking, most delightful user experience
  • Workday Hero Award — The idea that quietly makes everyone's workday better
  • Crowd Favorite Award — The people's champion, decided by community vote
  • J.A.R.V.I.S.'s Pick — The AI judge's favorite, based on build quality and Copilot telemetry

Every project is automatically entered into all six categories — there's no category to pick on the form.

What we want to see: thoughtful AI work that feels relevant to ORE, well executed, and worth celebrating in front of the wider team. New or recently built work is perfect; already-running production systems may also be considered on a case-by-case basis, provided the AI contribution is genuinely your team's own.

Submit Your Project

What to Submit

  • A completed SUBMISSION.md — the problem, your approach, technologies/models, setup, and value
  • The working project — source code, or a hosted demo / recording if it can't be run locally
  • Screenshots or a short demo wherever they help tell the story
  • Names of contributors (individual or team)
  • Your untouched, auto-generated Copilot usage files
